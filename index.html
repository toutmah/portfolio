Clutchain × Nalu

"Stop paying for a logo. Start paying for results."

Academic project · Brand campaign · Pair

PUBG Mobile × YETI

Role — co-author, two-person team

"Survive the Wild" — in-game survival unlocks real outdoor gear.

A limited-time collaboration between a mobile battle royale and an outdoor brand, argued commercially rather than on taste. Every choice is backed with data: PUBG Mobile over PC on reach, YETI over Stanley on thematic fit. The money is structured as a value exchange — a royalty to YETI on co-branded cosmetics instead of a flat fee — so both partners want the same outcome.

Academic project · Sponsorship pitch · Group

HOLY × Draculan

Role — team of four

A healthier energy drink entering esports through a Tier 2 event.

A sponsorship case putting HOLY — a low-sugar energy drink — behind Draculan, a Tier 2 Counter-Strike event in Romania. The argument is a cost-to-impact one: a Tier 2 event gives exposure to top teams and an international audience at a fraction of Tier 1 saturation and price, while opening a specific regional market.
